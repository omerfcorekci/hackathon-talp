# Cobalt Bank: client dossier

Fictional company. Sector: digital banking.

## Who we are

Cobalt Bank is a digital bank with a national bank charter, founded in 2019 and based in Austin, Texas. It has no branches. It offers checking and savings accounts, debit and credit cards, small personal loans, and card readers for small businesses. 640 staff, 180 of them in customer support. Its promise: "Banking with no surprises."

## A letter from the CEO, Elena Ruiz

> We grew fast on one promise: simple, fair, no surprises. The next ten weeks will test it. In December we move to a new core banking system. We have prepared for a year, but for a day and a half our customers will feel it. At the same time the board wants deposits to keep growing, and I know our customers compare savings rates every week. I do not want to buy loyalty with money we do not have, and I do not want to lose trust by staying quiet. Tell me what to do, and when.

## Where things stand on November 2

- 2.1 million customers; 1.0 million used the app in the last 30 days.
- Deposits $6.0 billion; about half of it belongs to 5% of customers.
- 310,000 customers have their paycheck deposited into Cobalt. Cobalt does not offer early pay: deposits show when the payer's bank sends them.
- 42,000 small businesses take card payments with a Cobalt card reader.
- App Store rating 4.6. About 1,800 complaints a month, mostly card disputes and transfer limits; about 120 a month reach the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB).
- Cobalt's savings rate is 0.5 points below the three best online banks.

## Our customers

> Mostly 22 to 40, living in cities and suburbs across the country: students, office workers, and some small business owners. Most also have an account at a big bank.

Groups you can simulate: everyday customers · paycheck customers · savers with large balances · small business owners with a Cobalt card reader · students.

## What the board wants by January 10

1. Customers active in the last 30 days: at least 98% of the November 2 number.
2. Deposits: at least 97% of November 2.
3. App Store rating at least 4.3, and CFPB complaints no more than twice the usual monthly number.
4. Both decisions together within $4 million.

The CFO cares most about goals 2 and 4, the marketing director about goal 1, the chief risk officer about goal 3.

## Red lines

- No false or misleading statement to customers.
- The migration date cannot move; the regulator has approved it.
- Rates and fees only at the levels in the menu.
- No layoffs and no closing products.

## Business as usual (what happens if you do nothing)

- Support answers in the app and by phone; the normal wait is 4 minutes.
- Card disputes follow the rules: provisional credit within 10 business days; the investigation can take up to 45 days.
- After any incident, a short holding statement goes out on social media within 24 hours.
- The migration notice goes out by email 7 days before.

## Decision menu (budget: $4 million for both decisions)

| Action | Levels and cost (CFO estimate) |
|---|---|
| Savings rate boost for 4 weeks, on balances up to $50,000 | +0.25 points: $0.8M · +0.5 points: $1.6M · +0.75 points: $2.4M |
| The same boost on new money only | half the cost above |
| Instant dispute credit: credit disputed card charges at once, up to $100 per customer, for 8 weeks | $0.4M |
| Free instant transfers to other banks (normally 1.5%, up to $15) | 2 weeks: $0.1M · 4 weeks: $0.2M |
| Debit card cash back for 4 weeks | 1%, up to $5 per customer: $1.2M · 2%, up to $10: $2.2M |
| Goodwill credit to a group you define, up to 100,000 customers | $5 each: $0.5M · $10 each: $1M |
| Early pay: show paycheck deposits up to 2 days early, from a week you choose, permanently | $0.5M (cost of funds and risk) |
| Zero-interest advance up to $500 for a group you define (up to 20,000 customers), repaid from their next deposit | $0.4M (expected losses and cost of funds) |
| Extra support staff for 4 weeks | +50: $0.25M · +150: $0.7M |
| Security pack: a free new card on request and an alert for every card payment | $0.3M |
| Higher offline card limit during the migration ($100 → $250 per payment) | $0.1M (fraud risk estimate) |

Messages: push, email and in-app messages are free. A text message to all customers costs $25,000 per send. A CEO video costs nothing but the CEO's credibility.

## What you know is coming

**Week 6: move to the new core banking system, Saturday, December 12, 00:00 to Sunday, December 13, 12:00 (Central Time).**
- For 36 hours the app is read-only: balances are visible, but no transfers, no peer-to-peer payments, no new cards.
- Card payments work offline up to $100 per payment.
- If something goes wrong, the switch can run "some hours" longer. Nobody can say how many.
- Many customers are paid on Friday, December 11, the day before.
- Rivals know the date too.
